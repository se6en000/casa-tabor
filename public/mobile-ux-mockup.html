Concept 1: Native App Shell

Resolves the "one long scroll" problem by introducing a persistent 5-tab thumb-zone navigation bar, a rapid-capture (+) FAB, and in-page segmented views (`Schedule`, `Triage`, `Kitchen`).

  • ✓
    Persistent Thumb Nav: Instantly switch between Today, Calendar, Action Hub, and Kitchen without scrolling.
  • ✓
    Segmented Switcher: Replaces 2,500px endless vertical clutter with crisp, focused sub-tabs.
  • ✓
    1-Tap Triage Action: Assign drivers and resolve reminders directly in 1 click.
  • ✓
    Center Elevated FAB (+): 2-click rapid capture for events, chores, and grocery items.
